Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work — like your profile information, the people in your circle, and the marbles you've added for them. We don't collect device or usage data.

How we use your information

We use your information to run the app's features, keep it secure and working reliably, and respond when you contact support.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We may share it with companies that help us host and run the app, and only under agreements that protect your data.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the reasons described above, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. When you delete your account, we delete your information, except we keep a record of your email address for administrative purposes.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your information. Reach out using the contact info below to make a request.

Children

Jar of Marbles is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at jarofmarbles@krautela.com.
