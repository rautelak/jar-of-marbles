About Jar of Marbles

Small acts become something bigger.

Jar of Marbles was created around a simple idea: connection grows through the small, repeated moments when people show care, keep their word, listen, and show up.

The idea

The name is inspired by the “marble jar” metaphor discussed by Brené Brown: trust is not usually built in one dramatic moment. It accumulates through many small actions over time.

Jar of Marbles turns that metaphor into a personal practice. It gives you a calm place to notice and remember the moments that strengthen your relationships and remind you of the kindness already present in your life.

Why we made it

Our attention naturally moves toward what is urgent, difficult, or missing. Jar of Marbles creates a small counterweight: a place to deliberately hold onto what was generous, thoughtful, loving, or simply good.

Jar of Marbles is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Brené Brown.
