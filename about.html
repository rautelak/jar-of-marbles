About Jar of Marbles

Small acts lead to something bigger.

The idea behind it is borrowed from Brené Brown's "marble jar theory" I came across a few years ago. The theory says "...trust is earned slowly through small, consistent actions rather than large, dramatic gestures."

Jar of Marbles takes that idea and turns it into a personal practice. It gives you a calm place to notice and share the moments that strengthen your relationships and remind you of the kindness already present in your life.

The App

Marbles: Any time someone in your circle shows up for you, listens, keeps a promise, respects your privacy, or is kind, they earn a marble. You drop a marble in their jar along with a message to let them know about the moment and what it meant to you.

Jar: This is where your marbles are stored anytime someone from your circle gives you one. Marbles are available to read right after they are dropped in your jar, but we recommend you resist the urge and let your jar fill up. This is why there are no notifications for incoming marbles. However, if you are running low and need a reminder of the kindness in your life, go ahead and draw a marble.

Circle: This is your circle of trust. Invite and add those who are close to you. If someone violates your trust, you can remove them from your circle. Only people in each other's circle can fill each other's jars.

The aim of the app is not to have to reach a full jar. Our aim is to make us pause, reflect, and recognize the kindness, love, and trust we receive every day in small doses while awaiting that one big gesture.

Once your jar is full, it's time to celebrate with your circle! Order a pizza, sit down, and share one marble at a time if you would like.

Why I created it

I believe love and trust don't arrive all at once. Our attention naturally moves toward what is urgent, difficult, or missing. Jar of Marbles creates a small counterweight: a place to deliberately hold onto what was generous, thoughtful, loving, or simply good even when it isn't immediately visible.

I'm an independent dreamer with a goal to give back more than I've taken from the world. Jar of Marbles is one small way I'm trying to do that: a place to notice and hold onto the kindness we so often overlook.
